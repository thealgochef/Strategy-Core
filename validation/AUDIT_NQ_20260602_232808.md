## 1. One-line verdict

**CONDITIONAL — the research *plumbing* is honest and reproducible, but the model shows *no
demonstrated deployable edge*.** Build Trade-Lab on this engine/contract with confidence; do **not**
deploy *this model* as alpha. On the only population it ever trades (NY-RTH, 0.70 gate) the full
out-of-sample edge is **11 trades over 9 months, +0.85 pts/trade net, 95% CI [−7.3, +9.0] —
statistically indistinguishable from zero**, with gated precision 0.545 ≈ a coin flip. The flattering
headline (0.805 precision, +7.5/+9.1 pt expectancy) is ~83% driven by **overnight** touches the
strategy never executes.

## 4. STILL DISHONEST OR UNVERIFIABLE (what would mislead you if you trusted the dashboard)

1. **The headline precision/expectancy is a blended, mostly-overnight number.** Dashboard precision
   0.805 (0.70 gate) and `expectancy_15_15` 7.53 mix 83% non-RTH touches. The deployment (RTH-only,
   0.70-gate) figures are precision **0.545** and net **+0.85 pt on 11 trades** — buried by the blend.
   `rth_fraction=0.17` is printed but easy to miss; nothing on the dashboard reports the RTH-only edge.
2. **`expectancy_15_30 = 3.80` is incoherent** (re-prices the sl=15 confusion at a 30-pt loss without
   re-resolving). It should be removed from the artifact, not shown next to the coherent 15/15 tile.
3. **`expectancy_15_15 = 7.53` is quoted at the 0.5/argmax gate, not the 0.70 inference gate.** Even
   the gate-correct *idealized* value (9.14) is not the deployable number; the honest RTH value is +0.85.
4. **The "70% rebound" base rate (the 1:1 thesis) is overnight.** RTH base rate is 0.525. Any sizing
   or expectancy that assumes the 70% prior is wrong for the execution window.
5. **`permutation_p = 0.004` is per-config, not family-wise.** Across the multi-config sweep, the
   selected config's effective significance is weaker; treat 0.004 as a within-config diagnostic only.
6. **"Some quality gates failed" then saved.** The fold-stability gate (std 0.161 ≥ 0.15) failed; the
   bundle was saved anyway. The single failing gate is the same instability the RTH small-n edge shows.
7. **Cost/CI honesty:** the only honest edge (RTH 0.70) has a 95% CI of [−7.3, +9.0] pt on n=11 —
   no statistical basis to call it positive. Treat as "edge not demonstrated," not "small edge."
